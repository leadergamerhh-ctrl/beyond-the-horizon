# Beyond the Horizon

A cozy pixel-art life sim. Move to Cedar Falls, find a job, open a shop, fish at sunset and make a home, alone or with up to 3 friends.

**Play it free in your browser:** https://leadergamerhh-ctrl.github.io/beyond-the-horizon/

- Works on desktop and phones. On a phone, use your browser's *Add to Home Screen* to install it full screen.
- Your saves stay on your own device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- Online co-op uses peer-to-peer connections (PeerJS, MIT License). Share the 5-letter invite code with a friend.
